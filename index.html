About me

Curious. Creative. Technical.

I’m Sayan Mondal, a Robotics & Automation engineering student at The Neotia University in West Bengal. I’m fascinated by the moment an idea stops being a sketch and starts to move, sense and react on its own.

My work sits where robotics, electronics, embedded systems and intelligent automation meet. I write code in Python and C/C++, wire up Arduino and ESP32 boards, and turn bold concepts into working prototypes, one experiment at a time. AERIS, my adaptive emergency-response robot concept, is where I explore stable, responsive mobility and a lifting mechanism built for real emergencies.

I learn by building: breaking problems down, testing, fixing and trying again. My goal is to engineer machines that are smarter, safer and genuinely useful to people, and I’m always open to new projects, teams and ideas.
